Interactive learning activities
These choices and feedback are scripts for future website interactions. In this PDF, the answer and explanation are printed beneath each scenario. These activities are not screening or diagnosis.

Activity 1 — Brain and behavior A person feels confident after alcohol but finds concentration difficult. Which statement is best? A. Confidence proves the person is safe to drive. B. Alcohol may impair judgment even if the person feels confident. C. Alcohol only affects the liver. Feedback: B. Alcohol can affect brain processes underlying attention, reaction time and judgment. [2]

Activity 2 — Substances and risk Which statement is accurate? A. Every psychoactive medicine is harmful when appropriately used. B. All substances have identical effects. C. Effects and risks depend on the substance and person. Feedback: C. Medicines may be beneficial in appropriate care, and different substances act through different pathways.

Activity 3 — Emergency recognition A fictional person cannot be awakened and has slow, abnormal breathing. Which response is safest? A. Let them sleep. B. Seek emergency medical help immediately. C. Give them coffee. Feedback: B. These can be signs of life-threatening poisoning or overdose. Do not delay urgent care. [7]

Activity 4 — Adolescents Why do adolescents need particular protection? A. Brain development is ongoing. B. They have no brain development left. C. Only adults can experience harm. Feedback: A. Brain development continues into young adulthood, and substance-related harms can affect learning and safety. [6]
