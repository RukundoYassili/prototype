Technology-Facilitated and Online Gender-Based Violence
Optional learning activities

Keep any reflections private. Nothing is submitted. You may skip any activity. These are educational drafts; original qualifications and safety notes apply. Avoid saving personal details on a device someone else can access.

Activity A - Recognize the behavior Task: Read the five cases and identify whether harassment, stalking, extortion, impersonation, or economic control may be present. It is acceptable to identify more than one form. Facilitator guidance: Possible answer: Case 1 harassment; Case 2 possible stalking/monitoring; Case 3 image-based abuse; Case 4 digital-economic coercion; Case 5 sexual extortion and child safeguarding.

Activity B - Safe communication check Task: Imagine a friend says their partner reads their messages. List two questions you might ask before sending advice or resources. Facilitator guidance: Suggested answer: "Is it safe for us to communicate here?" and "Would you prefer another time or safer channel?" Do not ask for proof or demand disclosure.

Activity C - Consent and image sharing Task: Discuss whether consent to take or privately send a photo also means permission to forward it to classmates or colleagues. Facilitator guidance: Suggested answer: No. Consent is specific; sharing with one person is not agreement for further distribution.

Activity D - Institutional response Task: Imagine a school receives a report of repeated gendered harassment. Identify three supportive institutional actions. Facilitator guidance: Suggested answer: confidential intake, protection against retaliation, and referral to a trained safeguarding focal point; plus appropriate platform/evidence procedures.

Activity E - Optional safety reflection Task: Without writing personal passwords, account names or experiences, identify one trusted professional or service that could help with digital abuse. Facilitator guidance: There is no single correct answer. Avoid recording sensitive details in a device or notebook if it could be accessed by someone else.
