Gender-Based Violence Against Children and Adolescents
Optional learning activities

Keep any reflections private. Nothing is submitted. You may skip any activity. These are educational drafts; original qualifications and safety notes apply. Avoid saving personal details on a device someone else can access.

Activity 1 — Map a safe support network For educators and caregivers: identify three roles, not named children: a trusted safeguarding lead, a trained health/service provider and a responsible protection contact. Verify the route between them.

Activity 2 — Practice supportive phrases In pairs, practice a response to an invented disclosure using four principles: listen, believe without investigating, explain confidentiality limits, and refer.

Activity 3 — Spot misuse of authority Read Scenario A. Identify the authority imbalance, secrecy request and possible benefit offered. Explain why the student bears no responsibility for the adult’s conduct.

Activity 4 — School safety audit Check whether an institution has a child-friendly complaint method, disability access, retaliation protection, staff boundaries and documented referral procedures.

Activity 5 — Digital safety choices Read Scenario C. List three steps that require safety assessment before acting: blocking, password changes and collecting digital evidence. Do not use real images or accounts.
