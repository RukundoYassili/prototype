Understanding Stress and Healthy Coping Strategies
VUGA NDAKUMVA | Version 1.1 | 9 November 2026 | Educational draft

Choose only the activities that fit your circumstances. This is an optional learning plan, not a prescribed treatment or a test of success.

Day Small, flexible practice 1 Notice one stress sign and one possible source of pressure. 2 Identify one thing you can influence and one thing outside your control. 3 Try brief grounding if comfortable; stop if distress increases. 4 Reach out to one safe person or helpful service, if appropriate. 5 Choose one small adjustment that supports sleep, food, movement, or rest. 6 Notice one recurring thought and practise responding without self-criticism. 7 Review what helped, what did not, and whether further support is needed.

Reflection questions

What do I notice first when I begin to feel stressed? Which pressures are practical problems, and which feelings need attention? What is one unhelpful response I use when overwhelmed? What could I try instead? Who, if anyone, is safe and reliable for me to approach? What sign would tell me that I should seek professional care?
