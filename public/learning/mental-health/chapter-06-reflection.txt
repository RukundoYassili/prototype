Understanding Trauma and Post-Traumatic Stress
VUGA NDAKUMVA | Version 1.1 | 9 November 2026 | Educational draft

This exercise deliberately does not ask you to describe a traumatic event. Skip any question that feels uncomfortable. You may reflect privately without writing anything down.

What is one situation in which I currently feel relatively safe? What is one neutral activity that helps me reconnect with the present? Who, if anyone, respects my boundaries and could offer practical support? Is there a daily task I could make easier by asking for help? Would it help to discuss symptoms or sleep concerns with a qualified professional?
