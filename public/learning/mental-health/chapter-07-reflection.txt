Optional reflection questions

What does support mean to you at a difficult time? Which basic routines or practical tasks might need more support after a loss? How might cultural practices help one person but feel uncomfortable to another? What phrases could you use to listen without pressuring someone to speak? If severe distress persists, which qualified health professional or facility could you contact?

Do not require learners to describe their own bereavement. They may answer using a fictional situation or choose not to participate.
