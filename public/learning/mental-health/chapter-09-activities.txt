Relationships, Social Connection, and Mental Wellbeing
Optional learning activities

Keep reflections private. Nothing is submitted. You may skip any activity. Avoid recording personal details on shared or monitored devices. The original educational-draft status and safety qualifications apply.

Guided educational activities

Activity 1 — My support map (10–15 minutes) On paper, draw three circles labelled “People I trust,” “Places or groups I may choose,” and “Professional services if needed.” Add only contacts you genuinely consider safe. Do not include identifying information if someone else may see the page. A blank circle is not a failure; it identifies a possible area to build gradually.

Activity 2 — Connection without pressure (7 days) Choose a low-risk, realistic action: send a greeting, attend one community activity, sit with a classmate at lunch, or reconnect with a trustworthy acquaintance. Afterward, note how comfortable it felt, whether the interaction was respectful, and whether you want to repeat it. Stop or adapt the exercise if it increases distress or risk.

Activity 3 — Listening practice With a willing and trusted partner, take turns speaking about a neutral topic for two minutes. The listener does not interrupt and asks one clarifying question. Then switch roles. Reflect on the difference between listening and immediately solving a problem. Do not use sensitive trauma disclosures for this exercise.

Activity 4 — Boundary sentence Write a simple statement that describes a limit without blaming another person: “I need some time before discussing this” or “I prefer not to share my account password.” You do not need to deliver it to someone if that would be unsafe.

Activity 5 — Community inclusion audit Think of a school club, sports group, or community event. Ask: Can people attend with limited money or transport? Are newcomers welcomed? Can someone leave without pressure? Are disability needs considered? Is private information protected? Identify one change that could make the activity safer and more inclusive.
